This repository contains the scripts used in the study **“Estimation of Carbon Emission Factors and Uncertainty in Low Hill Forests of Three Hydrographic Units in the Peruvian Amazon”**.

## Overview

The scripts support the processing and analysis of National Forest and Wildlife Inventory (INFFS) data for:

- Data quality control and cleaning
- Aboveground and belowground biomass estimation
- Necromass estimation
- Carbon stock estimation
- Emission factor estimation
- Uncertainty analysis

## Study area

The study evaluates low hill forests in three hydrographic units of the Peruvian Amazon:

- Putumayo Basin
- Interbasin 4977
- Medio Bajo Ucayali Interbasin

## Reproducibility

The scripts were developed using **R, and Google Colab**, together with structured spreadsheets, and were used to automate the calculations and improve the traceability and reproducibility of the estimates.

## Reference

This repository supports the methodology and results presented in the associated scientific article.
